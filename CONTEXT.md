# Professional Portfolio

This portfolio is a durable, evolving account of Albert Chang's professional direction and work. It is primarily a record for his future self, while giving recruiters and collaborators a clear way to understand the work.

## Language

**Professional Record**:
A curated account of Albert's work, decisions, values, objectives, and career direction over time. It tells a connected story rather than reproducing a resume.
_Avoid_: Resume site, personal archive

**Brand Line**:
A short, memorable phrase that expresses Albert's professional identity and personality. It complements rather than restates the fuller professional positioning statement.
_Avoid_: Thesis, mission statement

**Project**:
A body of work that serves as the portfolio's primary evidence of Albert's engineering practice and professional interests. A project may include narrative, media, technical artifacts, and interactive material appropriate to that work.
_Avoid_: Portfolio item, tile

**Featured Project**:
A Project selected for the homepage because it best represents Albert's current direction or range. Featured status is editorial emphasis, not a different kind of Project.
_Avoid_: Primary project, promoted content

**Project Collection**:
The complete set of public Projects documented by the portfolio, including those not featured on the homepage.
_Avoid_: Gallery, feed

**Project Page**:
The dedicated expression of one Project. It shares the portfolio's identity and core project metadata but composes narrative, media, and interactive evidence according to the needs of that Project.
_Avoid_: Project template, case-study clone

**Project Card**:
An image-led preview of a Project in the uniform collection grid. It always identifies the Project by title; pointer and keyboard interactions may reveal a short summary, while touch layouts remain intentionally concise and do not repeat a call to action on every card.
_Avoid_: Project Page, content tile

**Experience Timeline**:
A compact, most-recent-first record of Albert's professional roles. Each entry identifies the organization, role, location, and dates without interpreting the relationship between roles.
_Avoid_: Career story, job-description list

**Education Journey**:
The account of Albert's academic development, including formative decisions, leadership, and work rooted in educational settings. It is summarized in the professional record and explored in depth on its own page.
_Avoid_: Coursework list, academic resume

**Community-Centered Engineering**:
Engineering in which people affected by infrastructure meaningfully shape its development and its benefits, burdens, and trade-offs are made explicit.
_Avoid_: Community-focused branding, public relations

**Project Visualization**:
Visual or interactive evidence that helps a visitor understand a specific project, such as component motion, system behavior, trade-offs, or modeled outcomes. Its form follows the project rather than a site-wide chart template.
_Avoid_: Decorative animation, generic chart

**Visual Motif**:
One lightweight, restrained power line that connects the portfolio's otherwise distinct ideas. It gives them a recognizable visual identity while remaining soft, subtle, and secondary to the content.
_Avoid_: Theme

**Color Mode**:
A palette variant for the same site, beginning with a light mode and potentially expanding to dark mode later.
_Avoid_: Theme
