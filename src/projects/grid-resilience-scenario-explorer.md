## A deliberately limited demonstration

This ongoing Project is sample content only. Its placeholder scenarios demonstrate how interactive evidence can sit beside explanation without presenting fabricated engineering analysis.

## What to notice

The comparison preserves its labels and values in text. The optional enhancement changes which scenario is emphasized; it does not hide the underlying information.
